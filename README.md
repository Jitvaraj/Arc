🚀 Arc v1.0.0 — Initial Release

Your personal space to track progress, build habits, and stay organised.

Arc is a personal tracking and productivity space designed for students. It brings academics, habits, workouts, notes, and daily planning together in one simple place.

---

✨ Features

📊 Exam Marks Dashboard

- Keep Coaching and Tuition exam results separate.
- Track scores and percentages across subjects.
- View average scores, highest marks, and total exams.

🎯 Productivity & Habit Tracker

- Make simple daily habit check-ins.
- Keep track of your active streak.
- Review your activity over time.

🗓️ Calendar & Daily Planning

- Keep dates and plans organised.
- Schedule future workout sessions with notes.
- Review your activity through the calendar.

🏋️ Workout Tracker

- Record exercises and training details.
- Log weight, sets, and repetitions.
- Follow your workout progress.

📝 Notes

- Save study plans, personal tasks, and random ideas.
- Organise and revisit your notes.

💬 Quote Wall

- Save quotes that matter to you.
- Arrange and rank your favourites manually.

---

🔒 Data & Privacy

- App data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB.
- No cloud account is required.
- Your personal records are not synced to a server.
- Back up your data using the app's backup/export feature.

«Offline note: Saved data stays on your device. The current version loads some app libraries from external CDNs, so internet may be needed when loading the app.»

🌱 The Beginning

Arc v1.0.0 is the initial release — a starting point for tracking small steps, learning from progress, and staying consistent.
